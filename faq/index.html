Frequently asked questions

Common questions about CampusCare, focus sessions, and looking after your energy during the semester.

CampusCare is a student wellness and productivity portal. It brings a calm landing page, a Pomodoro focus timer, a daily wellness check-in, analytics, and this help page together so you can manage energy and study time in one place.

Open Focus Timer from the navbar. Pick a mode (typically 25 minutes of work, 5 minutes of short break, and 15 minutes of long break), set a simple intention, then start the timer. Work until it rings, then actually take the break— that is the point of the method.

Protect attention in short blocks instead of all-night marathons. Use the timer for one realistic task, keep your phone away during focus, and stop when the session ends. CampusCare is designed for consistent days, not perfect ones.

Use Wellness (Check-In) to log mood, energy, and stress in a few seconds. Over time the stress calendar and analytics views help you notice patterns— sleep, screen time, and study load—without turning self-care into extra homework.

Yes. CampusCare is built to work on phones, tablets, and laptops. On a smaller screen, use the menu button to reach Home, Focus Timer, Wellness, and FAQ. The help cards stack so they stay readable.

CampusCare is a study companion, not a clinical service. If stress feels unmanageable, talk to a trusted friend, a campus counsellor, or a local helpline. You can also use the Contact page on CampusCare for project feedback—not for emergencies.

Wellness resources

Small, practical reminders you can use alongside the focus timer and daily check-in.

SL Sleep

Sleep

Aim for a regular wind-down. Late-night scrolling often shows up the next day as higher stress on your check-in.

FO Focus

Focus

One timer, one task. Use 25-minute blocks on the Pomodoro page when lectures and assignments compete for attention.

ST Stress

Stress management

Name the load on your check-in instead of ignoring it. A short walk or a long break after two focus cycles is still productive.

HA Habits

Healthy study habits

Plan fewer tasks than you think you can finish. Consistency on CampusCare beats a packed list you will abandon by Thursday.

Need more help?

If you are stuck, tired, or unsure where to start, open a short reminder and pick one next step—a break, a focus session, or talking to someone you trust.

Take a kind next step

You do not have to solve everything in one sitting. Step away for a few minutes, drink water, then come back to a single Pomodoro on the Focus Timer.

If academic stress feels heavier than a study block can fix, reach out to a campus counsellor or someone you trust. CampusCare can support routines; people support you.
